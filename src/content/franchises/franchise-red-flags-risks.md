## Litigation and dispute patterns

Heavy Item 3 activity — especially franchisor-vs-franchisee patterns — deserves counsel and context. One old dispute is not the same as a culture of conflict. Ask how disputes are typically resolved and verify what the FDD discloses versus what owners whisper.

## Growth vs support mismatch

Units awarded faster than coaches, trainers, and field support hired is a classic stress fracture. Ask for support headcount trends versus franchise sales. If Discovery Day is all celebration and no capacity plan, notice that.

## Item 19 misuse

Item 19 can be a helpful starting point. It becomes a red flag when:

- Salespeople treat it as *your* guaranteed outcome  
- Subsets are cherry-picked without plain explanation  
- Verbal numbers contradict or “improve on” the written disclosure  
- You are discouraged from building your own [pro forma](/franchises/franchise-pro-forma)  

Never rely on earnings claims beyond the franchisor’s FDD — and even then, treat them as someone else’s history, not your future.

## Former franchisees and NDAs

If former owners are hard to find, uniformly silent, or report pressure to sign broad NDAs as a condition of exit conversations, raise your skepticism. Former-owner “why I left” themes belong in [validation](/franchises/franchisee-validation-questions).

## Other walk-away signals

- Territory that is “protected” in language but weak in practice  
- Mandatory affiliate purchases with no credible pricing discipline  
- Training that is short theater with no ongoing coaching  
- Pressure to sign before you finish owner calls or attorney review  
- Closures and transfers in Item 20 with no coherent story  
- Culture mismatch you keep trying to talk yourself into  

## How to use this page

1. Keep a running red-flag list per brand.  
2. Assign each item a next proof step (FDD cite, owner call, Discovery Day ask, counsel).  
3. Pre-commit deal-breakers so excitement cannot rewrite them at the hotel bar.  

Then decide with [attorney review](/franchises/franchise-attorney-decision).
