If diligence still looks solid after owners, numbers, and Discovery Day, pay for franchise-specific counsel before you sign. A general business lawyer who rarely touches FDDs is not the same tool.

This is process education — **not** legal advice. Hub: [complete guide](/franchises).

Related watches: [Franchise FDD Deep Dive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hcgVWaPVYwc) · [4 Biggest Mistakes Franchisees Make](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_3Z1gUwhLBE)

## When to bring counsel in

- You have a shortlist of one or two brands, not twenty  
- FDD and agreement drafts are in hand  
- Major diligence questions are answered enough that legal spend is justified  
- You are ready to hear “walk” without taking it personally  

## What counsel typically pressure-tests

- Franchise Agreement vs FDD summaries (renewal, transfer, termination, dispute resolution)  
- Personal guarantees, liquidated damages, non-competes  
- Territory language and carve-outs  
- Supply restrictions and affiliate dealing  
- Any addenda or state-specific riders  

Bring your red-flag list from [risks](/franchises/franchise-red-flags-risks) and open items from [Discovery Day](/franchises/franchise-discovery-day).

## Structure notes (high level)

Buyers often weigh LLC vs S-Corp vs C-Corp depending on tax goals, partners, and funding path (including retirement-capital designs that may require specific structures). Entity choice interacts with banking, guarantees, and [funding](/franchises/franchise-funding). Decide with your **CPA** and counsel together — not from a blog comment.

## Go / no-go framework

Write answers before the signing call:

| Gate | Pass looks like |
|------|-----------------|
| Fit | Meets requirements; no deal-killers from your [model checklist](/franchises/choose-franchise-model) |
| Leadership & support | Credible capacity to support *your* ramp |
| Numbers | Conservative pro forma clears debt service + owner needs with WC buffer |
| Validation | Owners like you would buy again (pattern, not one quote) |
| Legal | Counsel clears or you accept specific risks in writing |
| Lifestyle | You still want this life on a bad Wednesday |

If any gate fails, walking is a successful diligence outcome.

## After a “go”

- Finalize capital stack and insurance  
- Calendar onboarding and hiring plan  
- Keep emotional temperature low — build mode starts now
