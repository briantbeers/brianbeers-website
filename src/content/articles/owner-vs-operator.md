## Growth is a role decision

“Scale” means different things depending on whether you stay in the work or step up to vision and capital allocation. Naming the role you want is the first systems design choice.

## Owner path (in brief)

You focus on decisions that compound: people, scoreboard, capital, and relationships. Delivery quality still matters — but other people own more of the day-to-day.

## Operator path (in brief)

You stay close to customers and craft. Growth still happens — through capacity, productized services, and simple SOPs — without pretending you must leave the work.

## Freedom as a design constraint

Time off, location flexibility, team dependency, and cash predictability are constraints you choose. Write them down, then reverse-engineer the operating model. Educational framing only — customize in conversation.
