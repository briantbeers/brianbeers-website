## Start with fit, not hype

Buying a business is less about chasing a hot category and more about matching a vehicle to your capital, skills, and the life you want. This guide is educational — not an offer or earnings forecast.

## Four questions that narrow the field

1. **Capital** — What can you comfortably put in without stretching into stress?
2. **Lifestyle** — What does a good week look like for you and your family?
3. **Skills** — Where do you already create value — sales, ops, people, craft?
4. **Role** — Do you want to be close to the work (operator) or design the system (owner)?

Write honest answers before you browse models. Clarity here saves months of wandering.

## Compare vehicles, not vibes

Independent models, franchises, and existing acquisitions each trade brand systems, training, and control differently. Use [sample models](/models) and the [franchise hub](/franchises) as structure — then talk through fit.
