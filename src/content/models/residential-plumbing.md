## What this model is

Residential plumbing businesses handle installs, repairs, maintenance, and after-hours calls. This page is an educational placeholder to illustrate how model guides might look — not a promise of results.

## Who it can fit

- Operators who respect licensing and safety culture
- Buyers evaluating an existing plumbing book of business
- Owners building a brand around speed, clarity, and trust

## How work typically flows

1. Call / form lead
2. Dispatch and diagnose
3. Present options and price
4. Complete work and collect
5. Follow-up / membership opportunities (where appropriate)

## Questions worth asking

- Buy an existing shop or build new under a clear brand?
- How will I recruit and retain licensed techs?
- What is the mix of emergency vs. planned work in my market?
