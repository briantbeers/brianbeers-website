## What this model is

Residential roofing is a classic home-services business: diagnose need, propose scope, deliver the job, and earn referrals. Placeholder educational overview only — not a franchise offer or earnings claim.

## Who it can fit

- Owners who enjoy coordinating crews and vendors
- Buyers who want a vehicle with recurring storm/repair demand cycles
- Operators comfortable with local marketing and insurance conversations

## How work typically flows

1. Lead comes in (referral, storm, digital, partner)
2. Inspection and estimate
3. Contract and scheduling
4. Installation or repair
5. Follow-up and warranty communication

## Questions worth asking

- Do I want to own the brand or buy an existing book of business?
- How will I attract and retain reliable installers?
- What does local competition look like on price vs. trust?
