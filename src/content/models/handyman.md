## What this model is

Handyman businesses solve the “odd jobs” gap: drywall, fixtures, minor plumbing/electrical adjacent tasks (within license rules), punch lists, and small renovations. Educational placeholder only.

## Who it can fit

- People who like varied work and customer contact
- Buyers seeking a simpler operating model than large crews
- Owners who may start lean and expand into specialties later

## How work typically flows

1. Inbound request or recurring client
2. Scope and quote
3. Schedule and complete
4. Invoice and ask for reviews/referrals

## Questions worth asking

- Which jobs will I take — and which will I refer out?
- How will pricing stay simple for customers and profitable for me?
- Do I want a solo lifestyle business or a multi-tech brand?
