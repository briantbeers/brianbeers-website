## What this model is

Used clothing resale turns thrift, wholesale lots, and private inventory into listed products across marketplaces or a branded shop. Placeholder educational content — not investment advice.

## Who it can fit

- People with taste for product curation
- Buyers who prefer lighter physical operations than home trades
- Owners comfortable with online listing ops and customer service

## How work typically flows

1. Source inventory
2. Sort, clean, photograph
3. Price and list
4. Ship or fulfill locally
5. Reinvest into better sourcing

## Questions worth asking

- Will I compete on volume, niche, or brand experience?
- How will I manage returns and quality control?
- What channels (marketplace vs. owned site) fit my time?
