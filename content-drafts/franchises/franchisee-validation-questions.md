# Franchisee Validation Questions: How to Run the Calls

Validation is where brochure claims meet Tuesday afternoon reality. Talk to owners who run the business the way **you** plan to — same owner-operator vs GM model, similar market type, similar growth ambition.

This page teaches *how* to validate and shares a **short sample** of questions. The complete list stays gated. See also [due diligence](/franchises/franchise-due-diligence) and [franchisor questions](/franchises/questions-for-franchisor).

Watch: [DO NOT buy a franchise unless you do this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPzsjtOpm_I)

## Process that respects owners’ time

- Prefer scheduled 1:1s; some brands also run group calls (use both if offered)  
- Use FDD contact information for current and former franchisees where provided  
- Set an appointment — do not cold-call the store and ask for “the owner” mid-rush  
- Call people **outside** your target market when possible so you are not treated as a competitor  
- Sample widely — one glowing call and one angry call are both incomplete  

## Conversation tips

- Give a short, honest background on yourself  
- Be likable and specific; vague buyers get vague answers  
- Expect a range: some owners share a lot, some share little  
- Take notes on training, support, marketing, labor, ramp, and cash needs  
- Thank them; for especially helpful calls, a small digital thank-you is classy  

Former owners matter. If exit stories all require heavy NDAs, treat that as a signal — more in [red flags](/franchises/franchise-red-flags-risks).

## Sample questions (4–5 only)

1. **How long have you been in, and what does a typical week look like for you as owner?** (Full-time in the business, or managing a GM?)  
2. **What do you like most — and least — about this business?**  
3. **How would you rate initial training, ongoing training, and day-to-day support?**  
4. **How is the relationship between franchisees and the franchisor / leadership?**  
5. **Relative to your original plan, how is the business doing — and would you buy this franchise again?**  

Notice what is *not* here: a public dump of every margin, earnings, and cost worksheet question. Those belong in a fuller pack after you are serious — and any numbers you hear are **that owner’s story**, not your guarantee. ## How to interpret what you hear

- “Struggle” in year one can still mean “on plan” — ask relative to the plan, not vibes alone  
- Multi-unit ambition: ask whether they own or want additional units, and why / why not  
- Pattern > anecdote: three owners citing the same bottleneck beats one viral complaint  

Then feed themes back into [Discovery Day](/franchises/franchise-discovery-day) questions.
