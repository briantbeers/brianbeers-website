# Franchise Discovery Day: What to Look For (and How to Debrief)

Discovery Day is not a vacation with a logo. Go in with a checklist; leave with **proof**, not vibes. Debrief the same day — while details are fresh — using the sections below.

Prerequisites: [due diligence](/franchises/franchise-due-diligence), [validation](/franchises/franchisee-validation-questions), and a draft [pro forma](/franchises/franchise-pro-forma). Hub: [complete guide](/franchises).

No dedicated Discovery Day cut in the current library — cover via: [Franchise FDD Deep Dive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hcgVWaPVYwc) · [How To Choose a Franchise](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DpXzbrsxFLk)

## Before you go

- Write your must-haves and deal-breakers from the [model checklist](/franchises/choose-franchise-model)  
- List open questions from FDD and owner calls  
- Bring a partner or advisor if they share the risk  
- Capture names and titles of everyone you meet  

## Debrief framework

### 1) People
Who impressed you and why? Any gaps or turnover concerns on the support team?

### 2) Promises & proof
What was promised, by whom? What evidence did you see on site or in writing? What still needs proof?

### 3) Operations
Workflow, tech stack, bottlenecks. Staffing reality and labor availability in *your* market. How they measure and fix quality misses. Capacity by season or daypart.

### 4) Marketing reality
Channels that drive revenue today. Lead handling and speed-to-lead. How they track CAC. Where brand-fund dollars actually go.

### 5) Numbers vs FDD
What matched Item 19 themes? What did not — and why? Startup capex missing from estimates? Working-capital guidance realistic or light?

### 6) Territory & real estate
How territories are drawn and protected. White space near you. Pipeline, landlord leverage, TI expectations. Transfer approval norms.

### 7) Support model
Coach coverage ratio and cadence. Onboarding day-by-day plan. Which playbooks are battle-tested. Support response expectations.

### 8) Supply chain
Vendors, pricing control, single-source risk. Equipment lead times. Rebates and who receives them.

### 9) Culture & validation check
What owners said on or off the record. Any “say one thing, do another” moments. Context on recent lawsuits or closures.

### 10–13) Red flags, deal-breakers, open questions, go/no-go
List red flags without sugarcoating. Pre-commit deal-breakers you will walk from. Assign owners and deadlines for unanswered items. Write must-haves vs nice-to-haves to proceed.

### 14) Next 72 hours
Tasks, owners, due dates, updated decision timeline.

### 15) Confidence score
0–100 today. What would raise it 10 points this week?
